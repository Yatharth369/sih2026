01

👥 Team Formation

  • Each team must have 4–6 members.
  • Each team must include at least one female participant.
  • Each participant can be a member of only one team.
  • Each team must have one designated Team Leader.
  • The Team Leader will be the primary point of contact with the organizing committee.
  • All members must belong to the eligible college/institution as per the applicable eligibility requirements.
  • Members may be from different branches/years, subject to eligibility.
02

📝 Registration

  • Only the Team Leader should submit the registration form.
  • Submit the form only once per team.
  • Enter the correct name, enrollment number, branch, year, gender, email ID, and mobile number of every team member.
  • All details must match official college records.
  • Incorrect, incomplete, duplicate, or misleading information may lead to rejection or disqualification.
  • If any correction is required, contact the coordinator instead of submitting another form.
  • Registration will remain provisional until verified by the organizing committee.
03

💻 Problem Statement

  • Each team must select one SIH Problem Statement from the list provided by the organizers.
  • Enter the correct Problem Statement Number and Title.
  • Teams should thoroughly understand the problem before selecting it.
  • Teams should consider:
  • Technical feasibility
  • Dataset / API / resource availability
  • Innovation potential
  • Social / industrial impact
  • Ability to build a working prototype within the available time
  • The organizing committee may review the selected Problem Statement or ask a team to reconsider its selection.
  • Final Problem Statement selection will be subject to the internal evaluation process.
04

🤖 Solution & Innovation

  • Solutions must be original and developed by the team.
  • Plagiarism or directly copying another team's solution is strictly prohibited.
  • Teams may use:
  • Open-source libraries / frameworks
  • Public datasets
  • APIs
  • Pre-trained AI/ML models
  • Research papers & other legitimate open-source resources
  • Teams must understand and be able to explain everything used in their solution.
  • AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, etc.) may be used for assistance, but the final solution must be understood and owned by the team.
  • Significant external resources should be properly acknowledged.
  • Fake datasets, fabricated results, or misleading claims are not permitted.
05

🔐 Eligibility & Duplicate Checks

The organizing committee may verify:

  • Duplicate enrollment numbers
  • Duplicate team registrations
  • Team size
  • Female-member requirement
  • Student eligibility
  • Problem Statement selection
  • Accuracy of submitted information

⚠️ If the same enrollment number appears in multiple teams, the concerned teams may be placed on hold until the issue is resolved.

06

📊 Internal Evaluation

Teams may be evaluated based on the following suggested criteria:

Innovation & Originality
20%

20%
Prototype / Implementation
15%
Understanding of Problem
15%
Impact & Usefulness
10%
Presentation & Teamwork

The final evaluation criteria and weightage may be modified by the organizing committee / faculty.

07

🎤 Presentation & Demo

Every registered team should be prepared to present:

  • Problem and existing challenges
  • Proposed solution & Innovation
  • Technology and Architecture
  • Working prototype
  • Impact & Future scope

💡 Pro Tips

  • Build a functional prototype/MVP rather than presenting only an idea.
  • Every member should understand the project and their contribution.
  • Judges may ask technical or conceptual questions to any team member.
  • The evaluation panel's decision will be final.
08

🧑‍💻 Prototype

A prototype may include:

🌐 Web Application 📱 Mobile Application 🤖 AI / ML Model 🔧 Hardware Prototype 🌐 IoT System ☁️ Cloud-Based System 📊 Data Analytics Dashboard 🔗 API / Service 💡 Any Tech-Based Solution

The prototype should clearly demonstrate how the proposed solution addresses the selected SIH Problem Statement.

09

⏱️ Hackathon Conduct

  • Follow the official schedule and instructions.
  • Report / present at the assigned time.
  • Late submissions may be rejected.
  • Maintain professional and respectful behavior.
  • Harassment, discrimination, abusive behavior, cheating, or misconduct will not be tolerated.
  • Follow all college campus rules and instructions of the organizers and faculty.
  • Any attempt to manipulate the evaluation process may result in disqualification.
10

🚫 Disqualification

A team may be disqualified for:

  • Providing false or incorrect information
  • Duplicate registration
  • Violation of team-size requirements
  • Failure to meet the female-member requirement
  • Plagiarism or copying another team's solution
  • Fake credentials or fabricated results
  • Misrepresentation of the project
  • Serious misconduct
  • Failure to follow organizer / faculty instructions

The organizing committee / faculty will have the final decision regarding disqualification.

11

📩 Communication

  • The Team Leader will be the primary contact person.
  • Important announcements will be shared through the official communication channel.
  • Team Leaders are responsible for communicating information to their team members.
  • Regularly check official college communication channels.
  • Report registration-related issues to the coordinator within the specified time.
12

📋 Team Leader Declaration

Before submitting the form, the Team Leader must agree to the following declaration:

"The information provided in the registration form is true and accurate. All listed members have agreed to participate in the Internal SIH 2026 Hackathon, and the team complies with the specified eligibility and team formation requirements. I understand that providing false information, duplicate registration, plagiarism, or violation of the rules may result in disqualification."
