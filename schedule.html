DAY 01

10 September 2026

📍 TPO Office / Conference Room
⏰ 9:30 AM – 10:30 AM

Reporting, Attendance & Verification

All participants are required to report to the TPO Office by 9:30 AM.

  • Attendance of all participants will be recorded.
  • Team and participant verification will be completed.
  • A Verification Form will be provided to each team.
  • The form must include: Problem Statement, Problem Statement Description & Problem Statement Code/Number.

⚠️ Before arriving at the college:

Visit the official SIH portal and check the Problem Statement you have chosen along with its corresponding Code/Number. This is only to confirm and note down the correct code. No separate verification or approval is required on the SIH portal.

✅ After arriving at the college:

Enter the selected Problem Statement, its Description, and the corresponding Code/Number in the Verification Form provided by the organizing team.

⏰ 10:30 AM – 1:30 PM

Project Development & Hackathon Work

Teams begin working on their projects. Venue: TPO Office / Conference Room.

  • Problem analysis and solution planning
  • Coding and software development
  • Research and technical development
  • Hardware/IoT development and integration
  • Prototype development, testing and debugging
  • Documentation and project preparation
☕ 1:30 PM – 2:00 PM

Refreshment Break

  • Refreshments will be provided by the college.
  • Participants may use this period for a short break.
⏰ 2:00 PM – 4:30 PM

Project Development & Hackathon Work

Teams resume project work. Focus on:

  • Continuing implementation and improving the solution
  • Integrating hardware/software components
  • Testing the project and fixing technical issues
  • Preparing for Evaluation Round 1
🔍 4:30 PM – 5:30 PM

Evaluation Round 1 – Progress Evaluation

First evaluation by faculty members/mentors. Each team should explain:

  • Selected problem statement and the problem being addressed
  • Proposed solution and work completed so far
  • Current implementation and prototype/demo
  • Technical challenges faced and planned next steps
🏁 5:30 PM

Day 1 Closing, Progress Reporting & Checkout

Day 1 officially concludes at 5:30 PM. Before leaving:

  • Report work completed and communicate current project status.
  • Mention pending tasks for Day 2.
  • Ensure workspace is organized and cleared.
  • Complete required checkout formalities.
DAY 02

11 September 2026

📍 TPO Office / Conference Room
⏰ 10:00 AM – 10:30 AM

Reporting, Attendance & Verification

All participants must report by 10:00 AM.

  • Attendance will be recorded.
  • Team participation will be verified.
  • Any remaining administrative formalities will be completed.
⏰ 10:30 AM – 1:00 PM

Project Development & Hackathon Work

Teams continue working. Focus on:

  • Completing project development and finalizing implementation
  • Testing, debugging and improving the prototype
  • Resolving technical issues
  • Preparing the solution for Evaluation Round 2
☕ 1:00 PM – 1:30 PM

Refreshment Break

  • Refreshments will be provided by the college.
  • Participants may use this time for a short break.
⏰ 1:30 PM – 2:30 PM

Final Project Preparation

Teams prepare for Evaluation Round 2:

  • Completing pending development and final testing
  • Prototype/demo preparation and documentation
  • Reviewing technical details and preparing for judge questions
🔍 2:30 PM – 4:30 PM

Evaluation Round 2 & Shortlisting

Teams evaluated by the judging panel on:

  • Understanding of the problem statement
  • Quality of solution and technical implementation
  • Innovation, uniqueness and functionality
  • Feasibility, practical impact and overall execution

🏆 ~5–7 teams will be shortlisted for the Final Presentation Round.

🎤 4:30 PM – 5:10 PM

Final Presentation & Q&A Round

Shortlisted teams present to the judging panel:

  • Problem Statement and Problem Understanding
  • Proposed Solution and Technology & Implementation
  • Working Prototype/Demo
  • Innovation, Uniqueness, Impact & Future Scope

Judges may ask about architecture, scalability, real-world applications, and limitations.

🏆 5:10 PM – 5:30 PM

Final Judging & Winner Announcement

  • Judging panel will finalize the evaluation.
  • Scores/results will be consolidated.
  • Final winners will be announced.
  • Hackathon officially concludes at 5:30 PM.

Important Participant Guidelines

📋

Before the Hackathon

  • Verify your SIH Problem Statement and Code/Number in advance.
  • Keep all required documents ready.
  • Ensure every team member understands the selected problem.
  • Arrive prepared to begin work immediately after verification.
⏱️

During the Hackathon

  • Report strictly according to scheduled timings.
  • Maintain continuous progress throughout the event.
  • Keep project ready for evaluation at all times.
  • Follow all instructions from faculty, mentors, and judges.
  • Maintain professional conduct throughout.
🎤

Final Presentation

  • Have your PPT and working prototype/demo ready.
  • Every member must know the problem & solution.
  • Be prepared for technical implementation questions.
  • Know your technology stack and limitations.
  • Be ready to discuss impact and future scope.

🔧 Hardware / IoT Project Guidelines

Teams in Hardware/IoT projects are solely responsible for arranging all required hardware. The college will not provide project-specific components. Bring everything needed:

  • Sensors, Microcontrollers, Modules & Electronic components
  • Development boards, Cables, Connectors & Tools
  • Power supplies and other project-specific equipment
